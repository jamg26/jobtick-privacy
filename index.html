JobTick privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · Version 1.0.0 · Published at https://jamg26.github.io/jobtick-privacy/

JobTick is made by JAMGSOFT (contact: jammmg26@gmail.com). It records your work hours for clients and jobs on your Android device and turns them into timesheets you can export. This policy explains what JobTick handles, where it goes, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • Your clients, jobs, rates, hours and notes stay in JobTick's storage on your device. JAMGSOFT has no server and never receives them. There's no account.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking by JAMGSOFT.
  • A file leaves your device only when you save or share it (a PDF, a CSV or a backup), to the place you choose in Android's own screens.
  • Google Play handles the optional Pro purchase. The Google Play Billing library sends Google its own purchase and diagnostic data, described below.

What JobTick handles

Your work records. Client display names (no email, phone, address or contacts access), job names, hourly rates and currencies, rounding choices, report time zones, the start and end of your work and its pauses, notes, corrections and their reasons, and the reports you save. They're kept in an app-private database on your device.

Timing information. To measure a running session accurately after the app is closed or the phone sleeps, JobTick stores, with each stretch of work, the device's elapsed-time counter and its boot count (an Android setting that says how many times the device has started). They're used only to tell whether the phone restarted while the timer ran, and never leave the device except inside a backup file you make.

Settings. Your time display, first day of the week, default currency, duration display and, with Pro, the business name and logo you add to PDFs. The logo is copied, downscaled, into app-private storage.

Files you choose. When you restore a backup, import a CSV or pick a logo, JobTick reads only the file you choose in Android's document picker or photo picker. It doesn't ask for permission to read your storage or your photos.

The Pro purchase. Google Play processes payment. JobTick keeps Google's signed purchase receipt on your device, encrypted with an Android Keystore key and excluded from backups, and checks it on the device. JAMGSOFT never sees your payment details.

JobTick has no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK and doesn't use the advertising ID. JAMGSOFT doesn't sell or share personal data. JobTick works fully offline; the internet is used only by Google Play for Pro.

Backups

JobTick opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your records stay on this device. If you uninstall JobTick or lose the device, your records are gone unless you made a backup file: Settings → Back up now saves a JSON file wherever you choose. A backup file contains your records in readable form; keep it somewhere you trust. It never contains your purchase receipt or settings.

Deletion

  • Delete one entry or one job (and choose whether saved reports that include it are deleted too) from its screen.
  • Delete everything from Settings → Delete all data: every client, job, entry, saved report, draft and setting on the device. It can't be undone.
  • Uninstalling JobTick deletes all of its data on the device.
  • Files you exported or backed up are yours; delete them where you saved them.
  • JAMGSOFT holds no copy of your records to delete. For Google's purchase records, use your Google account.

Children

JobTick is meant for adults keeping work records. It isn't directed at children.

Changes

If JobTick starts handling data differently, this policy and the date above will change before that version is released.
